Our Services

Practical, client-centred support to help you do the things that matter to you.

Functional Assessment

The occupational therapy (OT) functional assessment aims to increase a person’s independence so they can take part in their community and complete meaningful tasks. When a medical condition or disability affects someone’s independence, they may be referred for an assessment to establish their current abilities, looking at their strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.

Therapy goals are then developed, either to build skills in areas of difficulty or to adapt the environment with assistive technology. We combine both approaches to achieve each client’s goals.

Assistive Technology (AT)

Assistive technology is the equipment, devices and software that help people with disabilities or limitations manage daily tasks, stay independent and improve their quality of life.

We use wheelchairs, prosthetic limbs, communication aids (such as hearing devices and speech-generating devices), memory aids and sensory aids to improve independence, increase accessibility and enhance quality of life.

Home Modifications

We work with our clients to modify bathrooms, kitchens, entrances and exits, bedrooms and living areas. This can include grab bars, easy-to-use appliances, lever-handled taps, adaptive cooking utensils, wider doorways, adaptive furniture, smart home devices, medical alert systems and telehealth equipment.

Home modifications increase safety, improve independence, enhance quality of life and can save costs. They are suited to older adults, people with disabilities, injured people and those with chronic conditions.

Housing Assessment

Functional capacity assessments designed to inform housing options for people with disabilities. They use both informal and standardised evaluations to understand each person’s specific needs. Our experienced OTs help you complete comprehensive housing assessments and reports so you can live in an accessible home that meets your needs.

Paediatric Occupational Therapy

Our paediatric occupational therapists identify children’s strengths and the areas where they may need support to take part in home and school life. We build gross and fine motor, thinking and communication skills, and help children learn basic self-care such as getting dressed, eating and brushing their teeth.

Telehealth (phone and video)

Our telehealth services provide accessible therapy support to NDIS participants from the comfort and safety of their own homes. Connect with our experienced occupational therapists from anywhere, on any device with internet access or phone reception, with the option of short or long appointments.

Telehealth offers the same personalised support as an in-person session and is more cost-effective. With no funding spent on travel, you can get more therapy time and make more progress towards your goals.
