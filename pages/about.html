About OTSteps

Helping clients reach their full potential.

Our story

OTSTEPS was born after our founder’s child was diagnosed with cerebral palsy.

We believe that understanding things from another person’s perspective is a difficult skill that is learned through personal experience. Empathy serves us well every day. Our experienced occupational therapists complete functional capacity assessments to understand what activities each client can engage in, their current limitations and their goals, and to find ways to enable them to take part in meaningful activities.

At OTSTEPS, the client is an integral part of the therapy process. Our goal is to ensure that every individual leads a fulfilling life.
