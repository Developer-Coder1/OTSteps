Our Services

Practical, client-centred support to help you do the things that matter to you, at home, at school and in your community.

Functional Assessment

We assess what you can do now, set clear goals with you, then build your skills or adapt your environment to increase your independence.

Assistive Technology

Equipment, devices and software, from wheelchairs to communication and memory aids, that help you manage daily tasks and stay independent.

Home Modifications

We work with you to make your home safer and easier to use, with changes like grab bars, wider doorways and easy-to-use fittings.

Housing Assessment

Assessments and reports that help identify housing options that are accessible and meet your needs.

Paediatric OT

Support for children to take part in home and school life, building motor, thinking and communication skills and everyday self-care.

Telehealth

Therapy by phone or video from your own home, on any device, with flexible appointment lengths and no travel.

What is Occupational Therapy?

Occupations are the everyday things that matter to you, such as cooking, driving, playing, working and caring for family. Occupational therapy is a client-centred health profession that helps you take part in those activities.

We assess what you can do now, what you want to do and what’s getting in the way. Then we adapt the task or the environment to fit you. Our approach is evidence-based, and you are always an active member of your own therapy team.
